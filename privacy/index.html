Privacy Policy

Floofy Bottoms LLC · A Maize in Mazes · Last updated November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play A Maize in Mazes (the “Game”) or visit floofybottoms.com (the “Website”), both run by Floofy Bottoms LLC (“we”, “us”), a small independent studio in California.

The short version: we don't run our own player accounts or servers and we don't sell your data. To make online play and voice chat work, the Game relies on Steam (Valve) and Epic Online Services (Epic Games), which handle certain information under their own privacy policies. The Unity engine sends us crash and error reports so we can fix bugs. Voice chat is live and is not recorded or stored by us.

1. Information the Game uses

Steam account information

You play through Steam. The Game uses your Steam ID and Steam display name to sign you in, show your name to other players, invite friends to parties, and record achievements and stats. Your purchase, payment and Steam account details are handled by Valve; we never see your payment information.

Epic Online Services (multiplayer and voice)

Multiplayer matchmaking, lobbies, connections between players and voice chat are provided by Epic Online Services (“EOS”). When you go online, EOS links your Steam account to an anonymous EOS player identifier and processes information needed to connect you, such as your Steam ID and display name, EOS player ID, IP address, approximate region, device and platform information, and session/lobby data. Connections between players may be routed through Epic's relay servers.

Voice chat

The Game has voice chat, which is on by default when you play online. Your microphone audio is transmitted in real time through EOS to other players: everyone in the lobby and on the results screen, and during a round, nearby players (free-for-all), your own team (team modes), or other spectators (once you're eliminated in free-for-all). We do not record, store or listen to voice chat. You can mute your microphone, lower or silence incoming voice with the Voice Volume slider, or mute individual players in Settings (individual mutes reset each time you start a new session). Please don't share personal information over voice chat; anything you say can be heard by other players.

Game progress and settings

Achievements and stats are stored with your Steam account. Settings and preferences (like volume or key bindings) are saved on your own computer. If you have Steam Cloud turned on, Steam may also back up some Game files to your Steam account so they follow you between computers.

Game engine (Unity) and crash reports

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may automatically receive limited technical information about your device (for example hardware, operating system and performance data). When the Game crashes, freezes or hits an error, Unity also collects diagnostic reports (such as crash logs and error traces) on our behalf, which we use only to find and fix bugs. This is handled under Unity's privacy policy. We do not use Unity Analytics, advertising SDKs, or any other analytics or tracking tools in the Game.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your real name, email address, home address, contacts, location (beyond the approximate region EOS uses for matchmaking) or payment details through the Game. The Game has no text chat, no ads and no in-game purchases.

2. If you contact us

If you email us for support, or to ask about press or business, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed.

3. The Website

floofybottoms.com uses no cookies, analytics or ad trackers, and our fonts are hosted on our own site. The Website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) for security and operation under GitHub's privacy statement. Embedded content from Steam (the store widget) and YouTube (our trailer, when available) is provided by those services and governed by their privacy policies.

4. Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. Information is handled only by the services the Game needs to work:

  • Valve (Steam): Steam Privacy Policy
  • Epic Games (Epic Online Services): Epic Games Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies (engine and crash reports): Unity Game Player Privacy Policy

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of players.

5. Where information is processed

We are based in the United States, and the services above may process information in the United States and other countries. By playing online, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in those places, under the safeguards those providers describe in their policies.

6. How long information is kept

We don't keep a player database of our own. Steam, EOS and Unity keep information according to their own policies. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Voice chat: mute your microphone, turn down incoming voice, or mute individual players in the Game's settings at any time.
  • Access or deletion: you can ask us what information we hold about you or ask us to delete it. Because most Game data is held by Steam, Epic and Unity, we'll delete anything we control and help you reach them for the rest. Include your Steam profile link so we can find your records.
  • California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, and residents of the EU/UK have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion and objection. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on providing the Game you asked for (contract) and our legitimate interest in running, securing and fixing bugs in online play. We will never treat you differently for using these rights.

Email floofybottoms.support@gmail.com with any request. We'll respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The Game is family-friendly, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Steam requires its own account age rules, and parents can use Steam Family View to limit online play. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

We rely on the security of Steam, Epic Online Services, Unity and GitHub, keep our own accounts protected with two-factor authentication, and hold as little information as possible. No online service is perfectly secure, but we'll tell you promptly if something goes wrong that affects you.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles information (for example, by adding new features), we'll update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be announced on our Discord and Steam page.
