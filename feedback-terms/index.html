We love hearing from players! These terms apply whenever you send us feedback, ideas, suggestions, bug reports or other comments about our games (“Feedback”), whether through the forms on this website, our Discord server, Steam discussions, email, or anywhere else.

1. Feedback is voluntary and not confidential

Sending Feedback is entirely optional. Please don't include anything you want kept private or confidential. We treat all Feedback as non-confidential.

By sending Feedback, you grant Floofy Bottoms LLC a free, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable right to use, copy, change, build on, publish and otherwise exploit that Feedback in any way, including in our games, updates, marketing and future products, without paying you, crediting you, or asking your permission. 3. We may already have the same idea

We're always working on new features, and other players may suggest similar things. If something in our games resembles your Feedback, that doesn't mean it came from you, and it doesn't create any obligation to you. We aren't required to read, use, respond to or keep any Feedback.

4. It's yours to give

You confirm your Feedback is your own, and doesn't include anyone else's confidential information or material you don't have the right to share.

Sending Feedback doesn't make you an employee, contractor, partner or co-owner of anything, and doesn't entitle you to any payment, royalties, credit or other compensation. If you have an idea you'd want to be paid for, please don't send it to us.
