4.How to enter

1. Record yourself (or your group) saying “A Maize in Mazes” three times, fast — video or audio.

2. Post it publicly on any public social platform (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Bluesky, Facebook, etc.) with the tag #amaizeinmazes3x. Your post and account must be public so we can view it from the link you provide.

3. Submit your entry on the official entry form (name/handle, email, platform, link to your post, solo/group, age confirmation, and the required agreements).

Limit: one official entry per person. Groups count as a single entry. Post as many clips socially as you like, but only one submitted entry per person is judged. Entries must be your own original recording. Entries that are incomplete, late, not publicly viewable, untagged, or that break these rules or a platform’s terms are void.

Keep it family-friendly. No unlawful, hateful, harassing, infringing, or explicit content, and no names or likenesses of people who haven’t consented. Sponsor may disqualify any entry at its discretion.

6.How winners are chosen

Phase 1 — Finalists. Sponsor’s panel reviews all valid entries and selects between three and ten finalists. The panel weighs several qualities together as a whole — no single one decides it: delivery (clearly saying it three times fast), creativity/originality, fun and personality, and following these rules. The panel’s selections are final.

Phase 2 — Public vote. Finalists are posted in the official Floofy Bottoms Discord, and the grand-prize winner is chosen by a public vote there using Discord’s built-in poll — one vote per Discord account. Anyone may join the server for free to vote during the voting window, and may vote based on whatever appeals to them. The finalist with the most valid votes wins.

Sponsor may discard votes it reasonably believes are fraudulent, automated, or manipulated, and may resolve any voting irregularities at its discretion. In a tie, Sponsor will choose among the tied finalists or hold a short run-off poll.
