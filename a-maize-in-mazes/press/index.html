Short description

A Maize in Mazes is a first-person multiplayer party racer for up to 20 players. Corn-cob characters sprint through see-through glass mazes toward a stick of butter they can see but can't easily reach.

Long description

The goal is always in sight. In A Maize in Mazes, up to 20 googly-eyed corn-cobs race through a brand-new glass maze every round, all heading for the same stick of butter. You can see it from the start. Getting there is the problem.

Sprint into a wall you didn't notice and you'll leave a face-smudge on the glass and stagger around dazed. Grab props to get ahead: spill Corn Oil to send rivals sliding, or lob a Cream Hurl (an egg) to slow them down. Proximity voice chat means you'll hear the panic around the next corner, and the gloating right behind you.

Play free-for-all or in teams of 2, 3 or 4, jump into a match with Quick Join or bring a party of Steam friends. There's also a solo mode for practice, plus achievements and mix-and-match player titles that show under your name for everyone to see.

A Maize in Mazes launches in Steam Early Access on November 6, 2026 for $3.99.

Features

  • Glass mazes, new every round. You can always see the goal; the path is the hard part.
  • Run into a wall at full speed and you leave a face-smudge and get dazed.
  • Race and sabotage with props like Corn Oil spills and Cream Hurls.
  • Up to 20 players: free-for-all or teams of 2, 3 or 4. Quick Join matchmaking and Steam friend parties.
  • Proximity voice chat.
  • Solo mode, achievements and mix-and-match player titles.
