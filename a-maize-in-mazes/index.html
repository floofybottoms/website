The cream of the crop

Easy to pick up, impossible to play without yelling.

See it. Can't reach it.

Every round is a brand-new glass maze. The butter is always in plain sight; finding the way there is the hard part. Hit a wall at full sprint and you leave a face-smudge and wobble around dazed.

Glass half full of corn.

Race & sabotage

Grab props to boost yourself or ruin your rivals' day. Spill Corn Oil for a slippery wipeout, or turn into Cobzilla and fling others into the glass.
